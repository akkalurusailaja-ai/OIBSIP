# EDA on Retail Sales Data
Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship - Level 1, Task 1

## What I did
- Data inspection and descriptive statistics (mean, median, mode, std)
- Monthly and quarterly sales trend charts
- Customer demographics (age groups, gender)
- Product category revenue analysis
- Correlation heatmap
- Extra chart: revenue by category and gender
- Observations after each chart and 3 business recommendations

## Key insights
- Q3 was the strongest quarter, August the best month
- 155 orders above 1,000 bring 49.4% of total revenue
- Women drive Beauty and Electronics, men lead in Clothing

## Files
- RetailSalesEDA.ipynb - full notebook
- OIBSIP_Retail_Sales.csv - dataset

Tools: Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, Google Colab
